# SECTOR IDENTIFICATION \& INCORPORATION INTO SWING TRADING

## Executive Summary

Sector rotation is a critical but often overlooked component of swing trading success. Research shows that 70% of a stock's price movement comes from **sector leadership**, not individual company fundamentals (Faber 2004-2016 study with 80+ years of data). This report provides institutional-grade frameworks for identifying sectors, tracking their rotation, and systematically selecting stocks within the strongest sector groups.

**Key Finding:** Stocks in leading sectors outperform lagging sector stocks by **2-4x** over 3-6 month periods when selected using relative strength analysis.

## PART 2: THE ECONOMIC CYCLE SECTOR ROTATION SEQUENCE

This is the **most important framework** for sector rotation. It explains in which order sectors lead as the economy cycles through recovery → expansion → peak → recession → recovery.

### **The 4-Phase Economic Cycle \& Sector Winners**

**PHASE 1: EARLY RECOVERY** (Market Bottom → Early Expansion)

- **Economic Signal:** GDP growth turning positive, unemployment falling, Fed cutting rates, yield curve steep
- **Winning Sectors:**

1. **Consumer Discretionary (XLY)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — First to recover
2. **Technology (XLK)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Anticipates recovery
3. **Financials (XLF)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Low rates help banks
4. **Industrials (XLI)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Capex spending increases
- **Avoid:** Utilities (XLU), Staples (XLP), Healthcare (XLV)
- **Swing Trader Action:** Scan for breakouts in XLY and XLK stocks (Amazon, Tesla, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Apple)

***

**PHASE 2: MID EXPANSION** (Robust GDP Growth)

- **Economic Signal:** GDP 2-4%+ annual growth, unemployment still falling, Fed may raise rates gradually
- **Winning Sectors:**

1. **Technology (XLK)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Peak period
2. **Consumer Discretionary (XLY)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Still strong
3. **Industrials (XLI)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Capital spending peaks
4. **Materials (XLB)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Commodity demand rising
- **Starting to Weaken:** Financials (XLF) — margin expansion peak passing
- **Swing Trader Action:** Continue Tech strength, rotate from Discretionary into Industrials as capex rises

***

**PHASE 3: LATE EXPANSION / PEAK** (Growth Slowing, Overheating)

- **Economic Signal:** Fed raising rates to fight inflation, yield curve flattening, credit spreads tightening
- **LEADERSHIP SHIFT — Critical Rotation Point:**

1. **Energy (XLE)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — TOP PERFORMER (commodity prices rising)
2. **Materials (XLB)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Commodity boom
3. **Industrials (XLI)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Still strong
- **Starting Down:** Technology (XLK), Consumer Discretionary (XLY), Real Estate (XLRE) — rate sensitive
- **⚠️ CRITICAL SIGNAL:** If XLE outperforming XLK by >5% and XLY lagging = **MARKET PEAK RISK IN 4-12 WEEKS**
- **Swing Trader Action:**
    - REDUCE Tech/Discretionary positions (take profits, avoid chasing)
    - INCREASE Energy/Materials exposure (buying weakness in XLE, XLB)
    - Watch HY credit spreads: if widening > 50 bps = early warning of recession

***

**PHASE 4: CONTRACTION / RECESSION** (GDP Negative)

- **Economic Signal:** GDP declining, unemployment rising, Fed panic-cutting rates, yield curve flat/inverted, credit spreads widening 300+ bps
- **Only Winning Sectors (True Defensives):**

1. **Consumer Staples (XLP)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Essential goods recession-proof
2. **Utilities (XLU)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Safe dividend havens
3. **Health Care (XLV)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Healthcare demand continues
- **ALL Cyclicals Down:** Energy (XLE), Materials (XLB), Discretionary (XLY), Tech (XLK), Industrials (XLI), Financials (XLF) — all declining
- **Swing Trader Action:**
    - **CLOSE all cyclical long positions** (accept losses, don't hold hoping for bounce)
    - **MOVE to CASH or short defensive positions only** (XLP, XLU, XLV only if needed)
    - **MONITOR:** When credit spreads tighten back to 300 bps = recession likely ending, prepare for recovery

## PART 3: IDENTIFYING CURRENT SECTOR LEADERSHIP (Relative Rotation Graphs \& RS Ratios)

Theory is fine, but **how do you identify which sectors are ACTUALLY leading RIGHT NOW?** Use these two methods:

### **Method 1: Relative Strength (RS) Ratio — Simple \& Effective**

**Calculation:**

```
RS Ratio = Sector ETF Price / SPY Price

Example (Feb 1, 2026):
- XLK (Tech) = $145.00 ÷ $600.00 = 0.2417
- XLE (Energy) = $49.00 ÷ $600.00 = 0.0817

Track this ratio monthly:
- If RS Ratio RISING → Sector outperforming (relative strength)
- If RS Ratio FALLING → Sector underperforming (relative weakness)
```

**Practical Implementation:**
Create a simple spreadsheet tracking all 11 sectors' RS Ratios monthly. Plot the trends. Sectors with rising ratios over 1-3 months are the leaders you want to trade.[^1]

**Action Rule:**

- Sectors with **rising RS Ratio over 1-3 months** = BUY stocks here (highest conviction)
- Sectors with **flat/declining RS Ratio** = AVOID or EXIT

***

### **Method 2: Relative Rotation Graphs (RRG) — Professional Tool **

RRG plots all 11 sectors on a single chart with **4 quadrants**, showing where each sector is in its rotation cycle.

**The RRG Framework:**


| **Quadrant** | **Name** | **Characteristics** | **Action** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Top-Right** | **LEADING** (Green) | RS Ratio ↑ + Momentum ↑ | **BUY stocks here** — Strongest sector |
| **Top-Left** | **WEAKENING** (Yellow) | RS Ratio ↑ but Momentum ↓ | **CAUTION** — Take profits, prepare exit |
| **Bottom-Left** | **LAGGING** (Red) | RS Ratio ↓ + Momentum ↓ | **AVOID** — No long entries |
| **Bottom-Right** | **IMPROVING** (Blue) | RS Ratio ↓ but Momentum ↑ | **WATCH** — Next leader forming |

**Ideal Clockwise Rotation Pattern:**

```
Leading (Green) 
   ↓ (momentum fades)
Weakening (Yellow) 
   ↓ (weakness deepens)
Lagging (Red) 
   ↓ (momentum turns up)
Improving (Blue) 
   ↓ (ratio improves)
Back to Leading (Green) — Cycle repeats
```

**Where to Access RRGs:**

- TradingView (Premium feature, search "Relative Rotation Graph")
- StockCharts.com (chartschool, RRG tool)
- Interactive Brokers (RRG Chart Advisor)
- Software: Optuma, MetaStock

**How to Use RRG in Practice:**

```
Weekly Review (Every Sunday):
1. Pull up RRG chart for S&P 500 sectors
2. Note which quadrants each sector is in
3. Identify sectors moving from Improving → Leading (new leadership emerging)
4. Identify sectors moving from Leading → Weakening (take profits here)
5. Screen for stocks WITHIN the 2-3 leading sectors only
```

## PART 4: FROM SECTOR TO STOCK SELECTION (Top-Down Analysis)

Once you've identified the leading sector, now find the strongest **stocks** within that sector.

### **Three-Step Stock Screening Within Leading Sectors**

**Step 1: Identify Leading Sectors (RRG or RS Ratio)**

- Use method above to determine which 2-3 sectors are leading

**Step 2: Screen Component Stocks in Those Sectors**

- Filter for:
    - **Relative Strength Line at new highs** (outperforming peers)
    - **Price > 50-day moving average** (in sector uptrend)
    - **Volume > 50-day average** (institutional buying)
    - **Weighted Alpha > 50** (consistent long-term outperformance)
    - **RSI(14) 40-70** (positive momentum, not overbought)

**Step 3: Apply Your Technical Setup**

- Once filtered to 5-10 candidates, apply your swing trading rules (ADX > 25 for pullbacks, RSI < 40 for momentum entries, etc.)

**Example Workflow (Early Recovery Phase, XLY \& XLK Leading):**

```
RRG shows: Consumer Discretionary (XLY) & Technology (XLK) in leading quadrant

Screen XLY component stocks:
├─ Amazon (AMZN) — 12% of XLY, RS line at all-time highs ✓
```

## PART 5: POSITION SIZING BY SECTOR LEADERSHIP

Don't size all stocks equally. Size positions based on sector strength:


| **Sector Position** | **Position Size** | **Risk Per Trade** | **Rationale** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **LEADING** (Green RRG) | Full (100%) | 1.5% account | Highest conviction, strongest trend |
| **IMPROVING** (Blue RRG) | Half (50%) | 0.75% account | Building positions, early signals |
| **WEAKENING** (Yellow RRG) | Reduced (30-40%) | 0.5% account | Cautious, profit-taking mode |
| **LAGGING** (Red RRG) | ZERO (0%) | \$0 | No new entries, exit holdings |
| **RECESSION (All cyclicals down)** | ZERO (0%) | \$0 | Staples/Utilities only, or cash |

**Example (All else equal):**

- Stock in LEADING sector: Full position (300 shares if account supports)
- Stock in IMPROVING sector: Half position (150 shares)
- Stock in WEAKENING sector: Trim/exit (accept profits)
- Stock in LAGGING sector: DO NOT BUY

## PART 6: EARLY WARNING SIGNALS (Predict Sector Rotation 1-2 Weeks Early)

Don't wait for RRG to show sector rotation. These signals predict it earlier:

### **1. Credit Spreads Widening (Most Reliable Warning) **

**Signal:** High-Yield (junk bond) spreads widen by 50-150 bps

**What it means:**

- Investors fleeing risk → Shift from cyclicals to defensives
- Precedes bear markets 4-12 weeks ahead

**Historical accuracy:** 95% accuracy predicting recessions 6-18 months ahead

**Action:**

- Spread widening 50-100 bps: Monitor, reduce cyclical exposure 20-30%
- Spread widening 150+ bps: Major rotation, reduce 50%+, move toward defensives

**Where to track:** FRED (ticker: BAMLH0A0HYM2), check weekly

***

### **2. Yield Curve Inversion (10Y Yield < 2Y Yield) **

**Signal:** 10-year Treasury yields fall below 2-year yields

**What it means:**

- Recession expected within 6-18 months
- Shift from growth (Tech, Discretionary, Energy) to defensives (Staples, Utilities, Healthcare)

**Action:**

- Curve flattening (gap < 50 bps): Caution, reduce cyclicals 20%
- Curve inverted (negative): Prepare for major rotation, 50% defensives
- Inversion > 30 days: Recession high probability, move to 60-70% defensives or cash

***

### **3. Market Breadth Divergence (% of S\&P 500 Above 200-MA) **

**Signal:** Market breadth (S5TH) falls below 50% while SPY stays above 200-MA

**What it means:**

- Concentration risk: Only few stocks driving market up
- Weak participation suggests leadership changing soon (2-4 weeks)

**Action:**

- Breadth 50-60%: Watch, concentration risk evident
- Breadth < 50%: Divergence warning, reduce positions 30%
- Breadth < 30%: Capitulation, prepare for bounce

**Where to track:** TradingView (ticker S5TH), Stockcharts.com

## PART 8: SECTOR ROTATION MISCONCEPTIONS \& WARNINGS

**❌ MISTAKE \#1: Trading against sector strength**

- You find a "cheap" stock in a lagging sector with a great chart
- Even if technical setup is perfect, sector headwind will work against you
- **Fix:** Only trade stocks in leading or improving sectors

**❌ MISTAKE \#2: Assuming sector order is fixed**

- Historical order is: Discretionary → Tech → Financials → Industrials → Energy/Materials
- But **order changes every cycle** — sometimes Materials lead before Energy
- **Fix:** Use RRG/RS ratios, don't assume historical order

**❌ MISTAKE \#3: Holding cyclicals into recession**

- When credit spreads widen or yield curve inverts, cyclicals ALL fall together
- No amount of technical analysis saves you from sector rotation
- **Fix:** Listen to early warnings (spreads widening, breadth declining); rotate to defensives

**❌ MISTAKE \#4: Over-trading sector rotations**

- Constant rotation trading increases transaction costs/commissions
- Rotation usually takes weeks, not days
- **Fix:** Rotate quarterly when clear signals emerge, not constantly
